# Historical scholarship and social data feasibility

Reviewed October 31, 2026 after Sam confirmed that no historical scholarship/offer or follower dataset is already available. This assessment identifies candidates; it does not establish that enough matched records exist to estimate a before/after effect. No paid access was purchased, provider was contacted, or historical athlete dataset was downloaded.

## Assessment

Historical recruiting covariates are available through documented sources. Scholarship selection outcomes and contemporaneous social reach remain the limiting data. Social Blade is a plausible provider of older Instagram counts, subject to paid access and account-specific coverage. Recruiting profiles can supply reported offers, but a complete dated offer history and reliable negative outcomes have not been established. The current project should retain its narrower valuation interpretation unless a feasibility pilot resolves these issues.

These are source assessments, not a scrape specification. Do not turn undocumented website internals into a purported supported API, purchase credits without authorization, or treat access to a profile page as permission to redistribute a bulk dataset. The existing CFBD/CBBD performance collector does not collect recruiting histories or Social Blade data; those collectors are not yet implemented.

## What still prevents the requested model

1. **Outcome and population:** A defined pool of plausible athlete-school decisions with verified offers and non-offers is absent. Public lists are usually lists of reported positives. Unlisted schools must remain unknown rather than being coded zero. A documented offer is also distinct from receiving aid or choosing a college.
2. **Decision dates:** Recruiting-class years, commitment dates, signing dates and first scholarship offers are different. Dates near July 1, 2021 require actual event evidence. Class 2022/2023 recruits may have received offers before NIL, so class year alone cannot assign treatment.
3. **Social measurement:** Each follower count needs a verified athlete account ID/handle, observation date, platform, evidence and relationship to the decision. Counts measured after a decision introduce look-ahead bias. Current counts and interpolations from current counts cannot recover the missing past.
4. **Comparable covariates:** Current college statistics and final recruiting ratings can postdate the decision. Historical ratings or high-school metrics need their own observation dates, consistent definitions and coverage across sports/periods.
5. **Usable overlap:** A source with many offer records and a source with many social histories are insufficient unless the same recruits can be joined at the right dates. Coverage may favor famous recruits and differ by period. Report the intersection and exclusions before fitting anything.
6. **Inference:** Even complete before/after data would estimate a change in association under this design. Platform growth, COVID, recruiting/transfer changes and the separate 2025 policy change can confound a causal interpretation.

## A bounded feasibility pilot

First specify the recruit frame independently of social fame: sport, sex/competition scope, recruiting cohorts, schools or recruit-rating range, and the intended outcome. One manageable diagnostic is 20 recruits, five in each sport-by-period cell. This is a coverage check, not an inferential sample-size justification.

For each sampled recruit, record source identity, public profile URLs, known handle history, source access status and whether the following can be substantiated: an outcome, its exact date, at least one social observation before that date, and athletic covariates observed by that date. Choose the maximum acceptable lag before looking at counts and report it; do not opportunistically pick a later snapshot because it is more complete. Retain failures and reasons. Do not replace difficult pre-NIL recruits with famous ones to improve coverage.

Inspect a small number of public recruiting records and archived pages before committing to a large scrape. If considering Social Blade, first establish the account-specific historical coverage and licensed export terms. No spend is authorized by this source assessment. If paid access later becomes authorized, use documented endpoints, private header credentials, raw-response provenance and recorded query dates.

Summarize a coverage funnel separately by sport and period: selected recruits → verified identities → dated outcome evidence → pre-decision social evidence → eligible covariates → usable joined records. Document any loss caused by missing negative outcomes. Only then design the full sample and power analysis.

## If the pilot fails

Keep the existing current NIL valuation study as the available analysis, explicitly labeled observational and subject to its data audit. Alternatively, a question about **reported offer counts** or **commitment destination** may be feasible with a redesigned outcome and sampling frame, but it would not directly answer scholarship award decisions. A study of NIL estimated valuations before 2021 would also need a comparable outcome definition; an absent valuation is not a zero-dollar observation.

This assessment does not show that the original question is impossible. It shows that a complete dated and joinable dataset has not yet been established, and identifies exactly what must be demonstrated before the pre/post regression can be run.
